Overview

Lower River trades some comfort for remoteness. You float the lower Onon by drift boat, starting in tent-tipi camps before moving to gers further down.

One day is spent on foot — wading and walking water the boats can’t reach.

Why anglers choose it

  • Remote lower Onon water
  • Tent-tipi camps, then riverside gers
  • A wade-and-walk day on foot
  • Small group of 4, two anglers per guide

Day by day

Itinerary

11 days, Ulaanbaatar to Ulaanbaatar. Exact camps depend on water levels.

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Ulaanbaatar

    Met at the airport and transferred to your hotel in Ulaanbaatar.

  2. Day 2

    Charter flight to the Onon

    Fly to the river and your first tent-tipi camp.

  3. Days 3–9

    Float the lower Onon

    Fish new water every day — tent-tipi camps first, then gers, with one wade-and-walk day.

  4. Day 10

    Back to Ulaanbaatar

    Charter flight back to Ulaanbaatar and a night in the hotel.

  5. Day 11

    Depart

    Transfer to the airport for your flight home.

Where you stay

Tent-tipi and ger camps

Tent-tipi camps on the first stretch, then traditional gers lower down — all set up before you arrive.

Our camp team cooks every meal on the river. All meals outside Ulaanbaatar are included.

On the Onon

Target fish

Every fish is released: single barbless hooks, rubber nets, fish kept in the water.

Taimen

The world’s largest salmonid — our signature fish, always released.

Lenok

Often called Mongolian trout — hard-fighting and widespread.

Amur pike

A fierce ambush predator of the slower water.
