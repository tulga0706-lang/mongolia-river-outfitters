Overview

Headwaters is for anglers who want the river at its wildest. You fish on foot, covering new water every day, while horses and camels carry camp between ger and wilderness tent sites.

It is our most demanding trip — daily walking on uneven ground — and our most remote.

Why anglers choose it

  • Fish the upper river on foot, new water daily
  • Horse and camel support moves camp for you
  • Small group of 4, private trips up to 6
  • Catch & release with single barbless hooks

Day by day

Itinerary

13 days, Ulaanbaatar to Ulaanbaatar. Exact camps depend on water levels.

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Ulaanbaatar

    Met at the airport and transferred to your hotel in Ulaanbaatar.

  2. Day 2

    Fly to Murun, on to the upper river

    Flight to Murun in Khuvsgul, then overland to the headwaters and your first camp.

  3. Days 3–11

    Wade and walk the headwaters

    Fish on foot each day while horses and camels move camp between ger and wilderness tent sites.

  4. Day 12

    Back to Ulaanbaatar

    Return to Murun and fly back to Ulaanbaatar for a hotel night.

  5. Day 13

    Depart

    Transfer to the airport for your flight home.

Where you stay

Ger and wilderness tent camps

Gers where the trail allows, then wilderness tents further up — moved ahead of you by horse and camel.

Our camp team cooks every meal on the river. All meals outside Ulaanbaatar are included.

On the Delgermurun

Target fish

Every fish is released: single barbless hooks, rubber nets, fish kept in the water.

Taimen

The world’s largest salmonid — our signature fish, always released.

Lenok

Often called Mongolian trout — a regular, hard-fighting catch between taimen.

Grayling

Quick to rise to a small dry fly in the riffles and side channels.
