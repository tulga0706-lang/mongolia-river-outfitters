The river

Water, land and people

The Onon flows out of the Khentii mountains in eastern Mongolia and runs north-east into Russia, where it joins the Amur basin. That is why it holds fish you won’t find in the Delgermurun — Amur pike, asp and Amur trout share the water with taimen and lenok.

We fish it in autumn, when the larch turns gold and taimen feed hard before winter. Remote upstream water is reached by charter flight, and our three trips cover the river from the headwaters down — chain them together for an expedition of 20 days or more.

When to come

Climate & season

What makes it special

Landscape, wildlife, culture

01

Landscape

Broad valleys, golden larch and long views — the river changes with every bend and every week of the season.

02

Wildlife

Autumn brings birds on the move and animals along the banks — keep an eye on the hills between casts.

03

Culture

Eastern Mongolia has a long history on these steppes, and herding families still live along the valleys.

Fish of the Onon

Every fish is released — single barbless hooks, rubber nets, fish kept in the water.

The world’s largest salmonid — our signature fish, always released.

Lenok

Often called Mongolian trout — a regular, hard-fighting catch between taimen.

A fierce ambush predator of the slower water.
