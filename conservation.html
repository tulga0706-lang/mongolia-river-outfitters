Every fish goes back

Taimen grow slowly and live long. Since 2004 we have fished catch-and-release only — and record every fish so the rivers stay wild for the next twenty years.

ON THE WATER

Our catch & release rules

Every guide and guest follows them, on every trip.

  • Single, barbless hooks only
  • Minimum line and leader strength — spec from client
  • Rubber nets; fish stay in the water
  • Every fish measured and logged

SCIENCE, NOT ANECDOTES

Every fish recorded

01

Measured

Every fish is measured in the net, in the water, before release.
